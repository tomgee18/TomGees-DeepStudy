# Swift Alpaca Glow - AI Study Assistant

An AI-powered study assistant that transforms study materials into interactive learning tools. The application helps students and learners by:

- **Content Processing**: Upload and process PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD, RTF files (up to 10MB)
- **AI Study Tools**: Generate summaries, flashcards, quizzes, and mind maps from content
- **Interactive Chat**: Contextual AI chat about study materials
- **Multi-Provider AI**: Support for Gemini and OpenRouter APIs with 7+ models
- **Study Features**: Pomodoro timer, progress tracking, and personalized learning

## Target Users
Students, educators, and lifelong learners who want to enhance their study experience with AI-powered tools.

## Core Value Proposition
Transform passive reading into active learning through AI-generated interactive content that adapts to different learning styles.
